# Problem Statement 
## Integrated File Tracking and Workflow Management System for Academic Institutions

---

## Background

Academic institutions handle a large number of **physical and digital files** related to:

- Academics  
- Administration  
- Finance  
- Examinations  
- Student services  

These files often move across **multiple departments and authority levels**. Due to the absence of a **centralized tracking mechanism**, files frequently:

- Get delayed  
- Are misplaced  
- Remain stuck at intermediate stages  

This results in **inefficiencies, lack of accountability, and prolonged decision-making cycles**.

---

## Problem Definition

Design and develop a **centralized file tracking and workflow management system** that:

- Enables **transparent tracking** of files across departments and authority levels  
- Ensures **role-based access control**  
- Supports **timely alerts and escalations**  

The system should facilitate **smooth, accountable, and efficient institutional operations**.

---

## Scope of Work

### 1. File Lifecycle Tracking

The system should:

- Track files from **initiation to final resolution**  
- Maintain a **complete movement history**, including:
  - Departments involved  
  - Actions taken  
  - Timestamps  
- Display the **current status** of each file  
- Clearly indicate files that are:
  - Pending  
  - Delayed  
  - Stuck at specific stages  

---

### 2. Role-Based Access and Authority Levels

The system should support multiple user roles with defined access permissions, including:

- **Super Administrators**
  - Director  
  - Registrar  
- **Department-Level Administrators**
  - Head of Department (HoD)  
- **Operational Staff**
  - Office staff  
  - Clerks  
- **General Users**
  - Students  

Access control must ensure:

- Users can **only view or act on files** relevant to their role  
- Unauthorized access to sensitive files is prevented  

---

### 3. Departmental Workflow Management

The system should allow:

- Departments to **define and manage their internal workflows**  
- File operations such as:
  - Forwarding  
  - Approval  
  - Rejection  
  - Commenting at each stage  
- **Inter-departmental file transfers** with:
  - Complete audit trails  
  - Action history  

---

### 4. Alerts, Notifications, and Escalation

The system should:

- Generate alerts for:
  - Pending actions  
  - Approaching deadlines  
  - Prolonged inactivity  
- Support **automated escalation** when files exceed predefined time limits  
- Notify relevant stakeholders for **timely intervention**

---

### 5. Transparency and Reporting

The system should provide:

- Dashboards showing:
  - File statuses  
  - Bottlenecks  
  - Processing timelines  
- Department-wise performance monitoring for higher authorities  
- **Exportable reports** for:
  - Audits  
  - Administrative reviews  

---

## Expected Outcome

A **reliable, scalable, and secure file tracking platform** that:

- Improves **transparency and accountability**  
- Reduces **processing delays**  
- Enhances **institutional efficiency**  
- Enables **data-driven governance** within the academic ecosystem
